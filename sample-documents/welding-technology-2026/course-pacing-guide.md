# Introduction to Welding Technology — Course Pacing Guide

**Course:** Introduction to Welding Technology (CTE 1042)
**Grade levels:** 10–12
**Length:** One semester, 5 instructional days per week, 50-minute periods
**Prerequisite:** Principles of Manufacturing, or instructor approval

## Course description

An introductory course in welding processes, equipment and shop safety.
Students practise shielded metal arc welding and oxy-fuel cutting on mild
steel, learn to read basic weld symbols, and demonstrate safe handling of
compressed gases. The course emphasises hands-on booth time supported by
written safety verification before any student strikes an arc.

## Units in sequence

### Unit 1 — Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW) Fundamentals

**Length:** 3 instructional days
**Placement:** Weeks 1–2, immediately after the shop safety orientation

Students identify SMAW equipment, set amperage for a given electrode and base
metal thickness, and run a flat-position stringer bead on 1/4" mild steel
plate. Day 3 is a graded booth practical paired with the written assessment.

Key outcomes:

- Identify the parts of an SMAW machine and explain the function of each.
- Select an appropriate E6010, E6011, E6013 or E7018 electrode for a given
  joint and position.
- Set polarity and amperage within manufacturer tolerance for the chosen
  electrode.
- Run a flat-position stringer bead with consistent width and no visible
  undercut.
- Demonstrate correct PPE for arc welding, including shade selection.

### Unit 2 — Oxy-Fuel Cutting and Compressed Gas Safety

**Length:** 2 instructional days
**Placement:** Weeks 3–4, following Unit 1

Students assemble and leak-test an oxy-acetylene outfit, set working pressures,
and perform a straight freehand cut on 3/8" mild steel plate. Cylinder
handling, storage separation and flashback prevention are assessed in writing
before torch time is permitted.

Key outcomes:

- Assemble an oxy-acetylene outfit in the correct order and leak-test it.
- Set regulator working pressures appropriate to tip size.
- Light, adjust and shut down a cutting torch using the correct sequence.
- Perform a straight freehand cut with a kerf within 1/8" of the scribed line.
- State the required separation distance for oxygen and fuel gas cylinder
  storage.

## Assessment weighting

| Component | Weight |
|---|---|
| Booth practicals | 40% |
| Written unit assessments | 30% |
| Daily safety compliance | 20% |
| Shop maintenance rotation | 10% |

## Required safety gate

No student performs any cutting or welding operation until the written safety
assessment for that unit is passed at 100%. Missed items are re-taught the same
period and re-assessed before booth assignment. This gate is not waivable.

## Materials and consumables

Mild steel plate (1/4" and 3/8"), E6011 and E7018 electrodes in 1/8", oxygen
and acetylene cylinders under current hydrostatic test, striker flints, soapstone,
wire brushes, chipping hammers. See the equipment inventory workbook for
booth-by-booth condition notes and the replacement schedule.
