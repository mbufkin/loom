# When instructional graphs get revised after review

## Question

What does UbD, evidence-centered design, content-packaging practice, and educational KG literature say about **when** structure (lesson membership, assessment attach, material roles) should be revised **after** humans or review passes inspect documents — vs locking the org tree up front?

## Answer

Lock the **resource/atom inventory** and Stage-1-like priorities early; treat the **org tree as a hypothesis** and revise lesson membership, assessment attach, and material roles after humans or Path A/B/C-style review inspect the documents. UbD requires design-standards peer review and continuous Stage 2/3 alignment fixes; ECD expects iteration across layers when later inspection breaks earlier CAF wiring; IMS/SCORM separate revisable organizations from unordered resources; EduKG pipelines auto-assemble then HITL-refine before publish. For loom: Path A/B/C findings that retype, re-parent, split multi-lesson files, or re-role materials are **rebuild triggers**—do not freeze structure from first router dispatch.
