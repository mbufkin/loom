Breeds of Livestock: Cattle

 

Lesson Overview

Media: Microsoft® PowerPoint® Presentation (85 slides)

Seat Time: 7 Classes | 350 minutes teaching

 

Goal:

To provide students with the origins, physical characteristics, traits and usages of cattle breeds.

 

Description:

This lesson helps students discover valuable information regarding the types of cattle raised in the world, as well as their origins, physical characteristics, traits and usages. Students will learn the important roles and purposes cattle breeds have in livestock production.

 

Objectives:

1.    To identify breeds of cattle.

2.    To understand the origins and characteristics of cattle breeds.

3.    To analyze the importance of specific cattle breeds in livestock production.

 

Class 1

Class Overview:

Dairy Breeds Microsoft® PowerPoint® Segment

Action Plan

Vocabulary Handout

Key Concepts

Dairy Breeds Check for Understanding

Dairy Decisions Activity

 

Essential Questions:

1.    What general terminology is associated with cattle?

2.    What are the origins, physical characteristics, traits and usages of dairy breeds?

 

Step 1: Bell Ringer:

·       Display an image of a Holstein cow, Brahman cow and Angus cow. Have students consider the question “what differences do you see physically in these three breeds of cattle, and how may this impact their use in livestock production”. Lead a discussion of their responses.

Step 2: Distribute the Action Plan, Vocabulary Handout and Key Concepts.

·       The Action Plan lays out a list of tasks for students to complete during the lesson.

·       The Vocabulary Handout is a list of terms used throughout the lesson.

·       The Key Concepts is an outline which identifies the main ideas presented in the lesson which students can fill in to aid in note taking during the lesson.

Step 3: Show the Dairy Breeds PowerPoint® segment.

·       This segment is 24 slides long.

·       Be sure to utilize the Key Concepts for this segment of the lesson.

Step 4: Administer the Dairy Breeds Check for Understanding.

·       The check for understanding is a short review of the content presented in the segment.

Step 5: Students should complete the Dairy Decisions Activity.

·       Students will select dairy cattle breeds for the given scenarios.

Step 6: Exit Ticket:

·       Have students turn in the Dairy Decisions Activity.

Activity Overview

Dairy Decisions

Students will select dairy cattle breeds for the given scenarios.

 

Accommodations:

Have students work with a partner to complete the activity.

 

Modifications:

Have students only respond to two scenarios.

 

Extension:

Have students compare their answers with others and discuss the similarities or differences in their responses.

 

 

Trivia Game Exit Ticket

In teams, students will compete against one another in a game of trivia about dairy, Asian and African cattle breeds.

 

Accommodations:

Provide trivia questions both orally and in a written format for students to reference.

 

Modifications:

Have students write their responses to only half of the trivia questions asked.

 

Extension:

Have students come up with their own trivia questions to ask the other team.

 

 

Cattle Breed Flashcards

Students will develop a set of flashcards to practice their knowledge of cattle breeds and their characteristics.

 

Accommodations:

Provide students additional time as needed.

 

Modifications:

Have students complete flashcards for only five to seven breeds.

 

Extension:

Have students devise a review game utilizing their flashcards and play it.

 

Project Overview

Build a Breed

Students will create their own crossbred or composite cattle breed which would suit their local area well.

 

Accommodations:

Allow students to work with a partner.

 

Modifications:

Have students complete only half the required components of the project.

 

Extension:

Have students debate on which new breed is best suited to the local area.
